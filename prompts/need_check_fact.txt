# prompt: need_check_fact v1 (spec §7.6)
# Narrow per-fact question for the text model.
# Placeholders: {fact} {transcript}
A video shows something on screen. Here is one visual fact, and what was
said out loud around that moment.

Visual fact: {fact}

Spoken words: {transcript}

Question: is the information in the visual fact stated or clearly conveyed
by the spoken words? Someone listening without seeing the screen — do they
get this information?

Return ONLY a JSON object:
{"answer": "covered" | "not_covered" | "unsure",
 "evidence": "the exact spoken words that convey it, or empty string"}

Rules: "covered" needs the actual information in the spoken words, not just
a pointing phrase like "as you can see". If the spoken words only point at
the screen, the answer is "not_covered". If you cannot tell, say "unsure".
